2026-11-21 (about 17:25 SAST)

A shooting in Worcester, an official update on cash-in-transit arrests, and a school killing in Mhluzi were added from official and media sources. No headline quarterly figure changed.

Shooting in Worcester

Worcester, Western Cape

Established

At about 01:20 SAST on 21 November 2026, six people (four men and two women) were killed and three people were seriously wounded. Police opened six murder and three attempted-murder cases. No arrests had been announced at the time of the official notice. Identities were not released. Motive Not established.

Handles: @SAPoliceService, @eNCA, @IOL

National Commissioner on cash-in-transit arrests

South Africa

Established

The National Commissioner said 55 most-wanted cash-in-transit robbery suspects had been arrested since 1 July 2026; six of them were shot and killed in confrontations with police; more than 33 illegal firearms were recovered. This is the Commissioner's account, not a quarterly crime total.

Handles: @ewnupdates

  • Eyewitness News

Arrests after a school killing in Mhluzi

Mhluzi, Mpumalanga

Established

Mpumalanga police said six people aged 18–20 were arrested on 20 November 2026 and were due in Middelburg Magistrate’s Court on 23 November, on a charge of murder. A 21-year-old learner was killed. The deceased and the suspects are not named here.

2026-11-20 (about 06:25 SAST)

Day-one edition, compiled before the 09:00 SAST SAPS Q2 release: incidents from 17–19 November and a short official and media X pulse. Quarterly figures from this edition are on the other site pages and are not repeated here.

Six people killed in a home in Botshabelo

Botshabelo, Free State

Established

On the night of 18 November 2026, three gunmen tried to break into a home, then forced entry and opened fire. Five women and a child were killed; a 48-year-old woman was wounded. Police opened a docket at Boithuso station for six counts of murder and attempted murder and launched a manhunt. Private people are not named here.

Handles: @News24

Source:

  • News24, 19 Nov 2026, quoting Free State police spokesperson Brigadier Thandi Mbambo

Cash-in-transit robbery in Bloemfontein

Navalsig, Bloemfontein, Free State

Established

On 18 November 2026 at about 11:20, explosives were used against a cash-in-transit vehicle. Early news reports, while a chase was still under way, said four people had been arrested and that no injuries had been reported at that stage. The official SAPS account later the same evening said seven people had been arrested, and that getaway vehicles, rifles, ammunition and cash had been seized.

Handles: @SAPoliceService, @IOL, @TimesLIVE

Source:

  • IOL, 18 Nov 2026
  • TimesLIVE, 18 Nov 2026
  • SAPS official post, 18 Nov 2026, 22:57 SAST

Woman shot dead in her home in Phoenix

Phoenix, KwaZulu-Natal

Established

A woman was fatally shot inside her home on 17 November 2026 (reported 18–19 November). Police told Daily Maverick the case involves unknown suspects, that the circumstances will form part of the investigation, and that no arrests had been made at the time of that report. Motive Not established. The deceased and other private people are not named here.

Handles: @dailymaverick

Source:

  • Daily Maverick, 18 Nov 2026, updated 19 Nov 2026
