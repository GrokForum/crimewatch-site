Cabinet statement on second-quarter crime statistics

South Africa

Proven

In a statement issued on 27 November 2026 about the 25 November Cabinet meeting, Cabinet said murders declined by 3.5% between 1 July and 30 September 2026 compared with the same period in 2025, truck hijackings dropped by 27.9%, and robberies at residences and businesses both declined by 7%. These are the SAPS counts of recorded crime as Cabinet quoted them. The murder decline of 3.5% is already on the published pages of this site. The truck-hijacking and robbery figures Cabinet quoted are official SAPS counts, but they are not shown on the published pages.

Source:

  • South African Government

Madlanga Commission hears evidence on illicit-tobacco cases

South Africa

Alleged

On 25 November 2026, former SARS commissioner Edward Kieswetter told the Madlanga Commission there is a gap between SARS, the police, the Hawks and the NPA. He pointed to more than 80 SARS investigations into illicit-tobacco syndicates, and said that in 2025/26 SARS handed 80 illicit-tobacco cases to the NPA, “with no progress to my knowledge.” He argued that the state should follow the money and freeze assets, not only make arrests. This is testimony to a commission and has not been tested.

Source:

  • Daily Maverick

2026-11-27 (about 05:55 SAST)

Parliament’s Police Committee said detectives met 40% of their targets in the first and second quarter performance reports, with about 300 dockets for each station detective. No headline quarterly figure changed.

Police Committee on detective performance

South Africa

Established

On 26 November 2026, Parliament’s Portfolio Committee on Police said SAPS detectives met 40% of their targets in the first and second quarter performance reports, and that each station detective carries about 300 dockets. The committee also said the Forensic Science Laboratory had not reduced the number of exhibits past the 90-day processing period. This is the committee’s account of the department’s performance reports, not a quarterly crime total.

Source:

  • Parliament of South Africa

Established
