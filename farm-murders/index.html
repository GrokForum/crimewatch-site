Farm murders

Back to CrimeWatch SA

SAPS does not have a standalone crime category called “farm attack” or “farm murder”. Official counts flag qualifying incidents on farms and smallholdings used for agriculture under the Rural Safety Strategy. Civil-society organisations keep their own registers, on their own definitions and calendars. The series are shown side by side and are not added together. There is no official farmer population denominator, so per-100,000 farmer rates are not calculated here. Not established as official per-farmer rates.

Official SAPS figures

Proven In July–September 2026, SAPS recorded 8 attacks on rural communities, against 12 in July–September 2025 (official PDF, slides 31–32).

Proven Murders of the farming community (victim linked to a farm or smallholding) in the same quarter: 8 (2 farm owners, 2 residents, 3 employees, 1 security guard). By province: Eastern Cape 2, Free State 1, Gauteng 2, Mpumalanga 1, North West 2; KwaZulu-Natal, Limpopo, Northern Cape and Western Cape 0. That is 8 of 5,590 national murders. Source: official Q2 PDF, slide 32.

Proven In the previous official quarter (April–June 2026), the official rural-community murder flag was 1 (a resident, Gauteng) of 5,427 national murders.

Proven Official SAPS data for July–September 2025 (Q2 2025/26), from an Agri SA-hosted copy of the SAPS PDF, with publisher SAPS (saps.gov.za): 12 rural-community murders out of 5,794 national murders (0.21%). The saps.gov.za original of that earlier pack was not swapped in during this pass. Q2 2025/26 PDF (third-party host).

Proven Ministerial replies give a longer official series of rural-community murders: 69, 50, 51, 55 and 44 in 2020/21 to 2024/25 (NW2957 and NW1027). That is 55 of 27,621 national murders in 2023/24 (0.20%, NW2957) and 44 of 25,573 in 2024/25 (0.17%; 25,573 from the SAPS briefing). Victims confirmed as farmers: 12, 14, 13 and 14 in 2020/21 to 2023/24 (NW2957). SAPS does not publish victim race. April–December 2025: 25 (NW1027). NW2957 · NW1027.

Alleged TLU SA count, unaudited, own definition: 27 murders, 1 January–7 October 2026, published via OFM on 8 October 2026. The count is TLU SA’s, not OFM’s. TLU SA’s own release of that year-to-date figure was not found in this pass. TLU also stated 32 murders in 2024 and 23 in 2025. OFM, 8 October 2026.

Proven as what TLU SA’s cumulative register PDF states. TLU SA cumulative register (August 2025), unaudited, own definition, no farmer denominator: 6,600 attacks and 2,309 murders, 1990–2025. The 2025 rows in that file are partial (14 murders and 93 attacks). TLU SA register PDF.

For calendar 2025 the published series already diverge (AfriForum 29; TLU 23; official SAPS April–December 25). Definitions differ. They are not added together.
