Heat map: recorded crime by province, police district and station

Official SAPS counts for Jul–Sep 2026 against Jul–Sep 2025, from the RAW Data sheet of the official Q2 2026/27 workbook. Choose a crime type, then click a province to zoom in to its police districts and stations. Province shading is a rate per 100,000 people, calculated from SAPS counts and the SAPS population series printed in the same pack (arithmetic; the murder and rape rates match the official ratios). Proven Source: SAPS, Q2 2026/27 workbook (RAW Data sheet); SAPS, Police Recorded Crime Statistics, Q2 2026/27 (Jul–Sep 2026), PDF

Levels: province, police district and police station only. No street, farm or victim location is mapped. Gang violence, Political killings, Farm attacks: no station-level SAPS data in this release, so those layers are shown empty, not as zeros. Province figures for those crimes are on the high-profile page.
