Proven Murder, rape and kidnapping counts are from the official Q2 Excel summary and sum to the national totals; sexual-offences counts are the official current-quarter provincial totals from the same workbook. Official murder and rape rates are from PDF slides 21 and 43. Kidnapping rates are not printed as official ratios in the Q2 pack. They are arithmetic from the official provincial counts and the official slide-21 populations: (count ÷ population) × 100,000, rounded to one decimal. Example: Gauteng 2,600 ÷ 17,438,410 × 100,000 = 14.9.

Eastern Cape has the highest official murder rate (16.2) and the highest official rape rate (23.3). Gauteng has the most murders (1,418) and 52.7% of recorded kidnappings (2,600 of 4,934), but a below-average murder rate (8.1) and the lowest official rape rate (10.1). Limpopo has the lowest official murder rate (2.6).
