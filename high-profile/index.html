High-profile crimes

One entry per high-profile crime type. Official counts and civil-society or survey figures sit side by side with their own labels and are never added together. Per-day figures are arithmetic from official counts (92 days in Jul–Sep 2026, 365 in 2025/26).

Murder

  • Proven 5,590 murders recorded by SAPS in Jul–Sep 2026 (−3.5% from 5,794). Previous quarter 5,427 (Apr–Jun 2026). Source: SAPS, Police Recorded Crime Statistics, Q2 2026/27 (Jul–Sep 2026), PDF; SAPS, Q1 2026/27 workbook
  • Proven 23,171 recorded in 2025/26; 25,573 in 2024/25; 27,590 in 2023/24 (SAPS briefing). Source: SAPS 2025/26 annual-report briefing to Parliament, 22 Oct 2026 (PMG)
  • Disputed For 2023/24 a ministerial reply gives 27,621 murders instead of 27,590. Both official figures are shown. Source: Written reply NW2957, rural-community murders; SAPS 2025/26 annual-report briefing to Parliament, 22 Oct 2026 (PMG)
  • Proven Per-day arithmetic: 60.8 (Q2 2026/27), 59.6 (Q1 2026/27), 63.5 (2025/26), 70.1 (2024/25). The often-quoted “about 85 a day” is above every recent official recorded daily equivalent. Source: SAPS, Police Recorded Crime Statistics, Q2 2026/27 (Jul–Sep 2026), PDF; SAPS 2025/26 annual-report briefing to Parliament, 22 Oct 2026 (PMG)

Rape and sexual offences

  • Proven 9,908 rapes and 12,444 sexual offences recorded by SAPS in Jul–Sep 2026 (−2.4% and −2.7%). 51,608 sexual offences recorded in 2025/26. Source: SAPS, Police Recorded Crime Statistics, Q2 2026/27 (Jul–Sep 2026), PDF; SAPS 2025/26 annual-report briefing to Parliament, 22 Oct 2026 (PMG)
  • Disputed “Only 1 in 9 rapes are reported” is not established as a current national rate. The primary paper usually cited (Jewkes & Abrahams 2002) is paywalled, so its exact wording and study area are unconfirmed; ISS Policy Brief 72 is only a secondary pointer to it. Source: Jewkes & Abrahams 2002, Social Science & Medicine 55(7) (paywalled); ISS Policy Brief 72, November 2014 (secondary pointer only)
  • Established SADHS 1998 (women aged 15–49, Table 5.12): 15.2% of women ever forced to have sex sought help from the police, about 1 in 7. Established for 1998 only. Source: SADHS 1998 full report (SAMRC)
  • Established The War at Home (Gauteng, fieldwork 2010): 1 in 25 of all rapes, and 1 in 13 of non-partner rapes, were reported to the police. Established for that Gauteng study only, not a current national rate. Source: Gender Links and MRC, The War at Home (Gauteng, 2010/11)
  • Alleged Rape Crisis estimate: 645,580 sexual offences a year with 7.7% reported, built from older research and 2017 recorded-crime figures. Not a current official total. Source: Rape Crisis Cape Town Trust, ‘The real numbers on sexual offences’
  • Established GPSJS 2025/26 cannot support a national unreported-rape total: Stats SA says sexual offences are undercounted in the survey, and its 22,000 individual incidences sit below the 51,608 already recorded by SAPS. Source: Stats SA, GPSJS 2025/26 Victims of Crime (P0341)

Gender-based violence

  • Proven Domestic-violence-related crimes flagged by SAPS, Apr–Jun 2026: 193 murders, 425 attempted murders, 871 rapes, 125 sexual assaults, 7,134 assault GBH and 14,392 common assaults. A flagged subset, not all GBV. Source: SAPS, Q1 2026/27 (Apr–Jun 2026), PowerPoint
  • Proven 2025/26 contact crime against women (18+): 199,145 (−2.2%); against children: 47,581 (+1.1%). Source: SAPS 2025/26 annual-report briefing to Parliament, 22 Oct 2026 (PMG)
  • Established GPSJS 2025/26: 408,000 individuals aged 16+ experienced assault; 70.3% of female victims said the perpetrator was known to them. Source: Stats SA, GPSJS 2025/26 media release
  • Proven 186 FCS units with a 27% vehicle shortage, described by SAPS as limiting their capacity to serve all stations. Source: SAPS 2025/26 annual-report briefing to Parliament, 22 Oct 2026 (PMG)

Kidnapping

  • Proven 4,934 kidnappings recorded by SAPS in Jul–Sep 2026 (+3.4%); Gauteng 2,600. Ransom demanded in 199 of a 4,637-count circumstances sample; 33 extortion-related; 15 human trafficking. Source: SAPS, Police Recorded Crime Statistics, Q2 2026/27 (Jul–Sep 2026), PDF
  • Proven 7,508 arrests for kidnapping in 2025/26 (7,543 in 2024/25). No standalone detection or conviction rate for kidnapping is published. Source: SAPS Annual Report 2025/26 (PMG copy)

Gang violence (Western Cape)

  • Proven In the SAPS Q2 murder causative-factor sample (5,396 of 5,590), 261 murders were classified as gang-related, 208 of them in the Western Cape (79.7%, arithmetic). Source: SAPS, Police Recorded Crime Statistics, Q2 2026/27 (Jul–Sep 2026), PDF
  • Proven Gang-related attempted murders in the sample: 232 of 271 nationally in the Western Cape (85.6%, arithmetic). Firearm murders in the instrument sample: Western Cape 732 of 2,552. Source: SAPS, Police Recorded Crime Statistics, Q2 2026/27 (Jul–Sep 2026), PDF
  • Disputed Western Cape murder total, Jul–Sep 2026: 1,109 (provincial summary), 1,110 (age-and-sex table) or 1,113 (sum of station rows). The release does not reconcile them. Source: SAPS, Police Recorded Crime Statistics, Q2 2026/27 (Jul–Sep 2026), PDF; SAPS, Q2 2026/27 workbook (RAW Data sheet)
  • Not established SAPS publishes no station table of gang-related murders, so no station-level gang layer is shown on the heat map. Source: SAPS, Police Recorded Crime Statistics, Q2 2026/27 (Jul–Sep 2026), PDF

Farm and rural violence

  • Proven SAPS Q2 2026/27: 8 attacks on rural communities (12 a year earlier) and 8 murders of the farming community (Eastern Cape 2, Free State 1, Gauteng 2, Mpumalanga 1, North West 2). Financial year to date (Apr–Sep 2026): 9 attacks and 9 murders (arithmetic from the two official packs). Source: SAPS, Police Recorded Crime Statistics, Q2 2026/27 (Jul–Sep 2026), PDF; SAPS, Q1 2026/27 (Apr–Jun 2026), PowerPoint
  • Proven SAPS Q2 2025/26: 12 attacks (p28) and 12 murders of the farming community (p29) of 5,794 national murders. Source: SAPS, Q2 2025/26 (Jul–Sep 2025), PDF
  • Proven Rural-community murders in ministerial replies: 69, 50, 51, 55 and 44 for 2020/21 to 2024/25. Source: Written reply NW2957, rural-community murders; Written reply NW1027, rural-community murders update
  • Alleged TLU SA’s own register (unaudited, own definition): 27 farm murders from 1 January to 7 October 2026; 32 in 2024 and 23 in 2025. Source: TLU SA statement, 7 Oct 2026 (own register)
  • Alleged AfriForum’s own register, calendar 2025: 184 farm attacks and 29 farm murders. Source: AfriForum, Plaasmoordeverslag 2025, 21 Apr 2026 (own register)
  • Alleged TLU SA cumulative register, 1990–2025 (2025 partial): 6,600 attacks and 2,309 murders. Source: TLU SA cumulative register PDF, Aug 2025
  • Established SAPS has no standalone ‘farm attack’ crime category; qualifying incidents are flagged under the Rural Safety Strategy. Official and civil-society counts use different definitions and periods and are not added together. Where independent counts exceed the official figure for the same period, the official figure appears lower, given those definitional differences. Source: Africa Check factsheet on farm-attack statistics, 2017 (method only); SAPS, Police Recorded Crime Statistics, Q2 2026/27 (Jul–Sep 2026), PDF

Political killings

  • Not established No official national count. SAPS said in a written reply that compiling political-killing cases since 2020 would need manual verification of individual case records. Source: Written reply NW4155, political-killing cases
  • Alleged GI-TOC’s open-source database: 45 political attacks and at least 43 people killed, 1 January to 30 September 2026. Reported incidents, not adjudicated killings. Source: GI-TOC, ‘Ballots and bullets’, October 2026
  • Alleged At the Q2 release the National Commissioner said the KwaZulu-Natal Political Killings Task Team has made 604 arrests since it was set up, with 151 accused sentenced; Gauteng’s team made 39 arrests in eight months and the Western Cape team 6 in three months. Official statements as reported; arrest totals, not counts of killings. Source: eNCA, political killings task teams, 20 Nov 2026

Rhino and other wildlife crime

  • Proven 352 rhinos poached in calendar 2025, down 16% from 420 in 2024; 230 in January–August 2026 against 248 in the same months of 2025. Source: DFFE, ministerial statement on 2025 rhino poaching; DFFE, World Rhino Day statement, 22 Sep 2026
  • Proven NPA conviction rate for environmental crime as a whole, 2025/26: 90% (483 of 537). Not a rhino-only rate; no rhino-only conviction rate is published. Source: NPA Annual Report 2025/26 (PMG copy; the NPA URL returned HTTP 403)
  • Alleged SANParks data released under PAIA and reported second-hand: 951 elephant deaths in Kruger 2020–2025, 179 of them from illegal causes. Source: Daily Maverick op-ed on Kruger elephant mortality, 8 Jul 2026
  • Not established No official national totals for abalone, pangolin, lion, elephant or cycad crime were found. Source: DFFE, World Rhino Day statement, 22 Sep 2026
