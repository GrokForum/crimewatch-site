CrimeWatch SA

South African crime figures, official and civil-society counts side by side, with victims first. Every figure links to its source and carries a fact label.

Latest official quarter (Jul–Sep 2026)

Counts below are what SAPS recorded or counted. They are not a census of every crime that occurred.

5,590

Murders recorded by SAPS, Jul–Sep 2026

Down 3.5% from 5,794 (−204). 5,590 ÷ 92 days = 60.8 recorded a day (arithmetic). Official ratio 8.8 per 100,000. Proven

Source: SAPS, Police Recorded Crime Statistics, Q2 2026/27 (Jul–Sep 2026), PDF

9,908

Rapes recorded by SAPS, Jul–Sep 2026

Down 2.4% from 10,154. 9,908 ÷ 92 = 107.7 recorded a day (arithmetic). Official ratio 15.5 per 100,000. Proven

Source: SAPS, Police Recorded Crime Statistics, Q2 2026/27 (Jul–Sep 2026), PDF

4,934

Kidnappings recorded by SAPS, Jul–Sep 2026

Up 3.4% from 4,772. Gauteng 2,600 (52.7%, arithmetic). Proven

Source: SAPS, Police Recorded Crime Statistics, Q2 2026/27 (Jul–Sep 2026), PDF

7,470

Attempted murders recorded by SAPS, Jul–Sep 2026

Up 3.0% from 7,255: this series rose while murder fell. Proven

Source: SAPS, Police Recorded Crime Statistics, Q2 2026/27 (Jul–Sep 2026), PDF

23,171

Murders recorded by SAPS, 2025/26 financial year

Down 9.4% from 25,573. Rate 36.72 per 100,000. 23,171 ÷ 365 = 63.5 a day (arithmetic). Proven

Source: SAPS 2025/26 annual-report briefing to Parliament, 22 Oct 2026 (PMG)

9.50%

Murder detection rate, 2025/26

SAPS-recorded detection on reported charges, against a 60% target (10.13% in 2024/25). Not a share of every murder that occurred. Proven

Source: SAPS Annual Report 2025/26 (PMG copy); SAPS 2025/26 annual-report briefing to Parliament, 22 Oct 2026 (PMG)
