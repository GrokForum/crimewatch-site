CrimeWatch SA

Official SAPS second-quarter crime statistics for July–September 2026 were released on 20 November 2026. This draft sets those official counts beside civil-society figures and survey estimates. Each factual claim is labelled separately. Official statistics record what was charged or counted, not everything that happened.

Proven Source of record: SAPS Police Recorded Crime Statistics, Q2 2026/27 (PDF). Companion Excel workbook. Landing page: saps.gov.za crime statistics. National counts below were checked against both the official PDF and the official Excel summary.

  • 5,590

    Murders, Jul–Sep 2026

    Proven Down 3.5% from 5,794 (−204 cases). That is 5,590 ÷ 92 days = 60.8 a day (arithmetic from the official count). Official ratio 8.8 per 100,000.

  • 9,908

    Rapes, Jul–Sep 2026

    Proven Down 2.4% from 10,154 (−246). That is 9,908 ÷ 92 = 107.7 reported rapes a day. Official ratio 15.5 per 100,000.

  • 4,934

    Kidnappings, Jul–Sep 2026

    Proven Up 3.4% from 4,772 (+162). That is 4,934 ÷ 92 = 53.6 a day. Gauteng 2,600, or 52.7%.

  • 8

    Farming-community murders

    Proven Official SAPS flag for Jul–Sep 2026, of 5,590 national murders. Civil-society registers use different definitions and are not added to this count. See farm murders.

A commonly quoted figure of about 85 murders a day is above the latest official daily equivalents: 60.8 in this quarter, and 63.5 in the 2025/26 year (23,171 ÷ 365, from the SAPS annual-report briefing). Not established The “about 85 a day” figure is not an official current rate in the packs used here.

Media compared with the official pack

This is a short note on the Q2 release coverage, not a census of all reporting.

factual_error The Acting Minister of Police’s official Q2 statement said that between 1 July and 30 September 2026 there were “over 1 600 fewer murders than the same period last year”. IOL and EWN reported that line. The official SAPS table released the same day shows 5,590 murders against 5,794 a year earlier, a decline of 204 (−3.5%), not 1,600. The 5,590 total in the statement and the reports matches the table; the year-on-year difference does not. Under the MediaWatch SA methodology, a misreported statistic is tagged factual_error. How the 1,600 figure was produced is Not established.

Reports: IOL, 20 November 2026 · EWN, 20 November 2026. Official source: SAPS Q2 2026/27 PDF.

IOL also wrote that the ministerial address did not provide the corresponding kidnapping figures. Proven The official pack does: 4,934 (+3.4%).

Open gaps

  • The NPA Annual Report 2025/26 PDF was still blocked (HTTP 403) when checked on 19 and 20 November 2026. NPA conviction rates used here come from a ministerial reply, not from that PDF.
  • Not established A national official series for political assassinations was not found in this pass.
  • Court-ready rates for murder, rape, kidnapping and rhino poaching were not found as standalone official rates.
  • Standalone detection and conviction rates for kidnapping, and a national conviction rate for rhino poaching, were not found.
  • Official provincial totals for wildlife crime other than rhino poaching were not compiled in this pass.
