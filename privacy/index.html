Privacy and corrections

Back to CrimeWatch SA

1. What this site is

CrimeWatch SA is a draft public site on recorded crime and related official and civil-society figures in South Africa. It aims to be neutral and sourced. It is not a police service, a court, or a newsroom, and it is not linked to SAPS, the NPA or any political party.

2. What it shows

It shows official recorded-crime statistics, official survey estimates, parliamentary replies, and civil-society or research figures that have been checked into the source register. Official and civil-society figures sit side by side and are not added together. Each factual claim is labelled with one of five terms from the MediaWatch SA methodology: Proven, Established, Alleged, Disputed, or Not established.

3. What it never shows

The site does not name private victims or private suspects. It does not publish home addresses, identity numbers, personal telephone numbers or personal e-mail addresses, family members who are not public figures, health information, or photographs of private people. Maps on this draft stop at province level. Where an official table ranks police stations, the station name is used; nothing finer is mapped.

4. Purpose

The purpose is to put official counts, survey estimates and labelled civil-society figures in one place so that they can be compared, with sources and dates, including where media reports and official tables differ.

5. What the site does not do

The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. It has no forms, no accounts and no scripts.

6. Corrections and removal

To ask for a correction or a removal, e-mail privacy@crimewatch.grokforum.site.

Please give the page link and the item you mean. Do not send identity numbers, telephone numbers or home addresses.

7. How long information stays

Each published build replaces the pages. Earlier versions may stay in the site’s public version history.

8. Who is responsible

The responsible party is GrokForum. Contact: privacy@crimewatch.grokforum.site.

9. Your rights

You can ask whether we hold information about you, and ask for a copy of it, by e-mail to privacy@crimewatch.grokforum.site.

You can ask for a correction of your information, or object to its processing, by the same address.

You can also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).
