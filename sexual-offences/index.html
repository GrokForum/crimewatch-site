Sexual offences and gender-based violence

Back to CrimeWatch SA

Reported police counts, survey reporting rates, and historical or civil-society estimates are shown separately. They are not multiplied together to invent a current unreported-rape total. Official statistics record what was charged or counted, not everything that happened.

Official recorded counts, July–September 2026

Proven SAPS recorded 9,908 rapes (−2.4% from 10,154) and 12,444 sexual offences (−2.7% from 12,787). Official rape ratio: 15.5 per 100,000. Arithmetic from the official rape count: 9,908 ÷ 92 = 107.7 reported rapes a day. Source: official Q2 PDF.

Proven Of 9,705 rape victims with verified sex and age in this pack (of 9,908), 4,362 were children under 18, 5,116 were adult women, and 227 were adult men (official PDF, slide 42). These are totals only. No private person is named.

Proven In the 2025/26 year, SAPS recorded 51,608 sexual offences (annual-report briefing). A separate annual rape total for 2025/26 was not found in the briefing tables used here. Not established for that annual rape total.

What surveys say about reporting

Stats SA’s Governance, Public Safety and Justice Survey (GPSJS 2025/26, P0341) asks households and individuals aged 16 and over about victimisation, including incidents never reported to the police. It is a household survey for a full year, not a police count, and is not reconciled here into a single “true” total.

Stats SA warns, in the same tables, that sexual offences are thought to be undercounted in GPSJS because of household interviews; people who already reported to the police are more likely to tell the survey officer; and the sample is too small for reliable disaggregation. The 22,000 individual incidences sit below the 51,608 sexual offences already recorded by SAPS in 2025/26. Because of that warning, this reporting rate cannot be inverted to produce a current official total of unreported rape. Not established as a current official unreported-rape total.

Sources: Stats SA P0341, GPSJS 2025/26; GPSJS 2025/26 media release.

Historical and civil-society estimates of underreporting

Each estimate below is labelled on its own. None is a current official national rate, and none is multiplied by the current SAPS rape count.

Not established as a current national reporting rate. A historical Gauteng-only research estimate, commonly cited as a “1 in 9” reporting ratio, comes from older South African Medical Research Council work. Later Gauteng fieldwork from 2009, summarised in 2015, found one in 13 for non-partner rape and one in 25 for partner rape in that province. Secondary citations used here are The Conversation (2015) and Wits CALS; the primary SAMRC paper was not re-opened in this pass. Africa Check has criticised the “1 in 9” ratio as outdated. Sources: The Conversation, 2015 · Wits CALS page on the rape-attrition study.

Alleged Rape Crisis Cape Town Trust estimate: 645,580 sexual offences a year with only 7.7% (one in 13) reported, built from older Gender Links work and 2017 recorded-crime figures. This is a civil-society estimate, not a current official total. Rape Crisis, “The real numbers on sexual offences”.

Case data from its stated period only, not extrapolated. Established as what that study reported about its sample. Tracking Justice (MRC / Tshwaranang / CSVR, published 2008) followed a sample of 2,064 rape dockets from Gauteng in 2003: 50.5% resulted in an arrest, 42.8% of those arrested were charged in court, trials started in fewer than one in five cases, and 6.2% of the original dockets ended in a conviction (about 1 in 20). These figures describe that 2003 Gauteng sample only. Wits CALS.

Domestic-violence-related counts and contact crime against women and children

Proven In the previous official quarter (April–June 2026), SAPS selected domestic-violence-related crimes included 193 murders, 425 attempted murders, 871 rapes, 125 sexual assaults, 7,134 assault GBH and 14,392 common assaults. These are counts flagged as domestic-violence-related, not all gender-based violence. Source: SAPS Q1 2026/27 pack.

Proven For 2025/26, contact crime against women (18+) was 199,145 (−2.2%); against children, 47,581 (+1.1%), from the SAPS annual-report briefing.

Proven GPSJS 2025/26: 70.3% of female assault victims said the perpetrator was known to them, including a spouse or intimate partner in 31.4% of those female cases (media release).
