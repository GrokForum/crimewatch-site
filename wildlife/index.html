Wildlife and poaching

Back to CrimeWatch SA

Human crime is the priority on this site; poaching and wildlife crime follow. Official rhino figures below are from the Department of Forestry, Fisheries and the Environment. SANParks (almost entirely Kruger National Park in these tables) is reported as its own row and is not added into a provincial total. Other wildlife-crime series (abalone, ivory, cycads and so on) were not compiled as official provincial totals in this pass. Not established as compiled provincial totals for those other crimes.

Proven Calendar 2025: 352 rhinos poached, a 16% decline from 420 in 2024 (266 on state property, 86 on private land). Kruger rose from 88 to 175, while KwaZulu-Natal fell from 232 to 97 (Hluhluwe-iMfolozi from 198 to 63, in the same ministerial statement). January–August 2026: 230 nationally, compared with 248 in the same months of 2025; Kruger 114, against 123.

Proven DFFE estimated a minimum national rhino population of 15,096 at the end of 2025 (at least 2,565 black and 12,531 white), a 4.9% increase since 2024, and said poaching in 2025 accounted for 2.2% of the population, below a 3.5% sustainability threshold cited in that statement.

Not established A national detection or conviction rate for rhino poaching was not found in this pass. Named accused in departmental case updates are not repeated here.

Sources: DFFE World Rhino Day statement, 22 September 2026; DFFE ministerial statement on 2025 rhino poaching.
